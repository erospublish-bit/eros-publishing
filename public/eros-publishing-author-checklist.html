The Manuscript-to-Publication Checklist

A practical checklist for independent authors preparing a book for professional publication. Use it to identify what is finished, what still needs attention, and what to discuss with your publishing partner.

1. Manuscript Readiness

  • Complete the manuscript draft.
  • Confirm the title or working title and author/pen name.
  • Review plot, structure, pacing, continuity, or nonfiction organization.
  • Complete the appropriate editing stage: developmental, copy editing, and/or proofreading.
  • Check names, dates, facts, quotations, permissions, and references.
  • Prepare front matter and back matter such as copyright, dedication, acknowledgments, author bio, and calls to action.
  • Keep a clean master manuscript and a backup copy.

2. Book Positioning

  • Define the primary genre/category.
  • Identify the intended reader.
  • Write a concise book description or synopsis.
  • Identify comparable books for positioning—not imitation.
  • Decide what makes the book distinctive.
  • Prepare an author biography and professional author photo if needed.

3. Cover & Interior Production

  • Choose intended formats: paperback, hardcover, e-book, and/or audiobook.
  • Select an appropriate trim size for print editions.
  • Finalize cover concept, typography, imagery, and back-cover copy.
  • Confirm print cover dimensions only after final page count and printer specifications are known.
  • Format the interior for readability and genre expectations.
  • Check chapter openings, headers, footers, page numbers, spacing, widows/orphans, and image quality.
  • Prepare separate files as required for print and e-book editions.

4. Publishing Setup

  • Decide who will be listed as publisher/imprint.
  • Determine ISBN needs for each applicable edition.
  • Prepare title, subtitle, contributor, description, category, keyword, and pricing metadata.
  • Select publishing and distribution platforms appropriate to the project.
  • Review each platform's current file and account requirements.
  • Confirm territorial rights and any permissions needed for included material.
  • Review retailer pricing, printing costs, and royalty structures before approving list prices.

5. Proof & Quality Control

  • Review digital proofs at full size.
  • Order and inspect a physical proof for print editions whenever practical.
  • Check cover alignment, spine, margins, image reproduction, page order, and blank pages.
  • Proofread the formatted book again—formatting can introduce new errors.
  • Verify links and navigation in e-books.
  • Confirm metadata and retailer descriptions before publication.
  • Approve final files only after corrections are complete.

6. Launch Preparation

  • Choose a realistic publication date.
  • Prepare author website, retailer links, and social profiles as appropriate.
  • Create launch graphics and book description copy.
  • Plan announcements, outreach, review requests, and promotional activity.
  • Keep final production files, source files, ISBN records, contracts, and account credentials organized.
  • Check live retailer listings after publication for accuracy.

7. Questions to Ask Before Hiring Publishing Help

  • Exactly which services and deliverables are included?
  • How many revision rounds are included?
  • Who owns the manuscript, cover, and finished files?
  • Who controls the publishing and retailer accounts?
  • How are third-party costs handled?
  • What is the estimated timeline?
  • What is not included in the quoted price?
  • What happens if the scope changes?
Remember: professional publishing is a sequence, not a single upload. Editing, design, formatting, metadata, proofing, distribution, and launch preparation each protect the quality of the finished book. No legitimate publishing service can guarantee sales, rankings, reviews, or earnings.
